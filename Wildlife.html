African Lion Prides

Pride social structures spanning 15–30 lions. Renowned for dawn and dusk ambushes across Central Serengeti plains.

  • Habitat: Open Grasslands & Kopjes
  • Best Sighting: July – October (Dry Season)

African Bush Elephant

Family matriarchal herds roaming beneath Mount Kilimanjaro and Tarangire ancient baobab corridors.

  • Habitat: River Valleys & Baobabs
  • Best Sighting: All Year Round

Zambezi Hippos & Crocs

Pods of 40+ hippos basking in shallow river pools above Victoria Falls and Mara River channels.

  • Habitat: Permanent Rivers & Lagoons
  • Best Sighting: May – November

Wildebeest & Burchell's Zebras

The greatest mass wildlife migration on earth, moving clockwise in a perpetual search for rainfall and sweet grasses.

  • Habitat: Short-grass Plains
  • Best Sighting: June – August (Crossings)

East African Cheetah

Accelerating from 0 to 100 km/h in 3 seconds across open plains to hunt Thomson gazelles during midday daylight.

  • Habitat: Flat Grasslands
  • Best Sighting: Dec – Apr (Ndutu Plains)

National animal of Tanzania, gracefully browsing thorny acacia treetops with prehensile 45cm blue-black tongues.

  • Habitat: Acacia Savannas
  • Best Sighting: Year-Round
